1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how personal and technical information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you access this website, sign in with a Google account, or use communication features provided through the service.

We are committed to transparency, data minimization, and providing free access to features without hidden fees, paid memberships, or commercial sale of personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the service, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Google Account Profile Information: When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your unique user identifier (UID), display name, email address, and profile photo URL as authorized by you during the Google sign-in prompt.
  • Account Activity Timestamps: Account creation timestamps and last-login timestamps used to maintain session continuity and protect account security.
  • Communication & Technical Metadata: Basic connection metadata, browser capabilities, and error diagnostics strictly required to operate real-time features reliably.

We do not collect payment cards, bank account credentials, billing addresses, or subscription payment details because this service does not operate any payment system.

3. Google Account Authentication

Authentication is powered by Firebase Authentication using Google as the login provider. Password entry and multi-factor verification take place exclusively on Google's secure authentication pages. This website never asks for, receives, or stores your Google password.

Firebase authentication persistence keeps your session active on your browser until you explicitly click Sign out or clear your browser storage.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate your account and keep you signed in across visits and devices.
  • Display your basic account profile (name, email, and profile photo) to you when signed in.
  • Maintain your user preferences, privacy settings, and saved account state.
  • Prevent unauthorized access, abuse, spam, and technical failures.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to privacy requests.

5. Account Information Storage

When you sign in with Google, your account profile is stored in Cloud Firestore under your private document path (users/{uid}), containing your UID, display name, email address, profile photo URL, account creation timestamp, and last-login timestamp.

Strict database security rules ensure that only you (verified by your authenticated UID) can read or update your private account document. Private account records are never exposed publicly to other visitors.

6. Camera and Microphone Permissions

This Privacy Policy page does not request camera or microphone permissions. If you choose to use video or audio features elsewhere on the service, your browser will prompt you for explicit camera and microphone permission before any media device is activated.

You may revoke camera or microphone permissions at any time through your browser or device settings.

7. Video and Audio Communication

Real-time video and audio sessions are transmitted over encrypted channels (such as WebRTC DTLS/SRTP) between participants or authorized relay servers when network traversal is required. Video and audio streams are not recorded by default.

8. Messages and User Content

If you send messages or publish user content through the service, that content is processed solely to deliver it to your intended recipients or audience according to the privacy controls you select. Do not share sensitive financial, government ID, or confidential personal data inside public or peer messages.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

Essential browser storage (such as IndexedDB and localStorage used by Firebase Authentication) is used to remember your signed-in state and basic preferences. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell browsing profiles.

10. Third-Party Services

This page relies on Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to provide secure Google Sign-In and private database storage. Google processes authentication data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase security terms. Third-party infrastructure providers may enforce their own service limits or quotas.

11. Data Security

We protect user data using HTTPS transport encryption, Firebase Authentication token verification, and zero-trust Firestore security rules that restrict document reads and writes to the verified document owner. While we apply industry-standard safeguards, no online transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

12. Data Retention

Account records stored under users/{uid} are retained while your account remains active so that you can sign back in on any supported device. Diagnostic or security logs, if generated, are kept only as long as necessary to maintain safety and reliability.

13. Account Deletion

You may stop using the service and sign out at any time. Users may request complete deletion of their account record and associated profile data stored in Firestore, subject to any minimal records required to be retained for legal compliance, abuse prevention, or security auditing.

14. User Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal account information stored about you (viewable directly when signed in on this page).
  • Request correction of inaccurate account details (automatically refreshed from your Google profile upon sign-in).
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions such as browser camera or microphone access.

15. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the higher minimum age required by applicable local law, including 18+ restrictions for adult live-video features). We do not knowingly collect personal information from underage individuals. If you believe an underage user has provided personal data, please contact us promptly so the account can be removed.
